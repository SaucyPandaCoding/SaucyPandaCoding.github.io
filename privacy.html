Effective and last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains how SaucyPandaCoding (“we”) handles information in Fossil Lab Tycoon and on this official support website. It does not cover our other games.

Current test versions: iOS 1.0 (3) and Android 1.0 (4) do not serve AdMob ads. Ad integration is being prepared for a later version. The advertising and age-choice sections below explain that planned functionality and apply when an ad-enabled version is made available.

1. Game information stored on your device

The game stores progress, currency, fossil and skeleton collections, upgrades, museum assignments, expeditions, request progress, offline timestamps and sound preferences in the app’s local storage. It may keep local recovery copies of saves made before migration or prestige. We use this information on your device to run the game, calculate progress while away and remember your choices.

The current game has no player sign-in, developer-operated cloud saves, chat or developer-run analytics service. It does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. Local save data is not automatically sent to a developer server.

2. Advertising in supported future versions

Ad-enabled versions may use Google AdMob for bottom banners and optional rewarded excavation boosts. An earned reward is recorded locally; closing an ad early does not earn it. Live ads are not enabled in the current test versions identified above.

When an adult player uses an ad-enabled version, Google’s Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform services may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or app identifiers, diagnostic and performance information, and ad interactions such as impressions, taps and video views. Google uses these for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention, subject to the configuration and privacy choices that apply. Non-personalized ads can still involve technical data processing; they are not the same as collecting no data.

The initial integration requests non-personalized, general-audience ads, does not request Apple’s tracking permission, and removes Android’s advertising-ID permission. Other app or device identifiers may still be used by the SDK. We do not provide your local game save, name or email to AdMob.

Where required, Google’s consent form appears before ad requests. Ads remain unavailable when the consent process fails or does not permit requests. Ad-enabled versions provide an Ad privacy choices control in Manage to reopen available privacy options. See Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and Google’s iOS and Android SDK data disclosures.

3. Children and age choices

Fossil Lab Tycoon has a mixed audience, including children. The initial ad integration uses a neutral age-group question, with no preselected answer and a “Prefer not to say” option. Only a coarse age-group choice is saved on the device; we do not ask for a birth date.

For this initial integration, players under 18 and players whose age is unknown receive no ads. The game does not start the advertising or consent SDK through its game code for those players. Adults may proceed through the applicable consent flow. Age settings are separate from gameplay progress. Any later change to child advertising will require a policy update and an appropriate app update before use.

Children should not put personal information in a support report. A parent or guardian can contact us about a child’s information using the support process below.

4. Support, testing and voluntary feedback

If you contact us through GitHub Issues, we receive the information you choose to post, such as your GitHub username, report text, device/version details and attachments. We use it to answer questions, fix problems and improve the game. GitHub Issues are public: do not post personal, sensitive or confidential information, full save files, passwords or receipts. Posting a report requires a GitHub account and is also governed by GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Apple may provide us with TestFlight testing metrics, crash diagnostics and feedback submitted by testers, including information attached to that feedback. Google or Apple may also process installation, store or device-backup information under their own policies. We do not control their storage or retention. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. This website

This is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising scripts, tracking pixels, forms or nonessential cookies to it. GitHub processes technical request information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security under its privacy policy. Following a link to GitHub, Apple, Google or another service takes you to that service’s own privacy practices.

6. Sharing and retention

We do not operate a data-broker business or sell local game saves or support reports. Public support posts are visible to others. Advertising providers, platform providers and hosting services handle the information described above under their own policies; they may process information in countries other than yours.

Local progress remains on your device until removed, overwritten or lost through app/device changes. Platform backups may retain separate copies. Support reports remain available for as long as the issue or related records are retained for support; GitHub’s history, caches and other copies may persist after a post is edited or removed. We may retain information when required to meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute. We cannot set retention periods for Apple, Google or GitHub.

7. Your choices and requests

  • You can control sound and other gameplay settings inside the game.
  • In ad-enabled versions, you can use the available privacy controls and your device’s privacy settings. Optional rewarded ads do not have to be watched to play.
  • You can delete local game data through your device’s app-data controls or by removing the app. This can permanently erase progress. We cannot restore a save from a developer server.
  • You can edit or delete your own GitHub support posts where GitHub permits, or ask us to remove information from a report we control.
  • Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of information, or to withdraw consent and contact a privacy regulator. Some requests must be directed to the provider holding that information.

8. Contact and policy updates

The publisher is SaucyPandaCoding. To contact us, visit our support page or open a general support/privacy inquiry on GitHub. Begin without personal information; do not put confidential details in a public issue. We do not need your game save or identity documents for a general privacy question.

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Material new data practices will be reflected in the relevant app’s notices and store disclosures before being enabled.
