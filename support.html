Player support

Let’s keep your lab running.

Fossil Lab Tycoon is developed by SaucyPandaCoding. Our support channel is the website’s GitHub Issues tracker. You can read existing reports without signing in; posting requires a GitHub account.

Reports are public. Do not post your email, password, account details, purchase receipts, full save files, or other personal information. Crop personal details out of screenshots. For privacy matters, start with a general request to contact the developer, without identifying information.

Report a problemBrowse known issues

What to include in a bug report

  • The game name, version and build number, if known.
  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • What you expected, what actually happened, and the steps to reproduce it.
  • A cropped screenshot or screen recording, if useful.

Saves and updates

Your progress, settings and local recovery backups are saved on your device. The game currently has no developer-operated cloud save or account system. We cannot retrieve a local save from our servers.

Update the existing app rather than uninstalling it. Deleting the app or clearing its data may permanently remove your progress. Device backups, where available, are managed by Apple or Google and are not a guaranteed game recovery method.

Some Android test APKs were signed with different temporary keys. If Android rejects an update, stop and report it. Do not uninstall your existing copy just to force the new APK to install.

iPhone / TestFlight

Use the TestFlight app to update an existing invitation. You can also submit feedback through TestFlight. Apple handles that feedback under its terms and privacy practices; check screenshots and feedback for personal details before sending.

Privacy questions

Read the privacy policy. To request clarification or removal of information you posted in our tracker, open a general issue or use the edit/delete controls on your own GitHub post. Do not submit confidential information in a public issue. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact us.
